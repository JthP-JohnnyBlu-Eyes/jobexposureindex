Questions about healthcare support jobs and AI

Will AI replace healthcare support jobs?

Not as a whole. The 19 healthcare support jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 24 out of 100 (moderate), #14 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which healthcare support jobs are most exposed to AI?

Medical Transcriptionists (88/100), Speech-Language Pathology Assistants (61/100), Pharmacy Aides (38/100).

Which healthcare support jobs are safest from AI?

Orderlies (0/100), Massage Therapists (7/100), Home Health Aides (8/100).

How many people work in healthcare support jobs?

About 12,317,220 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
