Questions about food preparation and serving related jobs and AI

Will AI replace food preparation and serving related jobs?

Not as a whole. The 16 food preparation and serving related jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 14 out of 100 (low), #17 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which food preparation and serving related jobs are most exposed to AI?

First-Line Supervisors of Food Preparation and Serving Workers (51/100), Chefs and Head Cooks (26/100), Cooks, Private Household (24/100).

Which food preparation and serving related jobs are safest from AI?

Dishwashers (0/100), Dining Room and Cafeteria Attendants and Bartender Helpers (0/100), Cooks, Short Order (0/100).

How many people work in food preparation and serving related jobs?

About 17,430,830 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
