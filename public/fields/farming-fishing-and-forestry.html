Questions about farming, fishing, and forestry jobs and AI

Will AI replace farming, fishing, and forestry jobs?

Not as a whole. The 12 farming, fishing, and forestry jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 13 out of 100 (low), #20 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which farming, fishing, and forestry jobs are most exposed to AI?

Agricultural Inspectors (29/100), First-Line Supervisors of Farming, Fishing, and Forestry Workers (28/100), Log Graders and Scalers (21/100).

Which farming, fishing, and forestry jobs are safest from AI?

Agricultural Equipment Operators (0/100), Fallers (3/100), Forest and Conservation Workers (4/100).

How many people work in farming, fishing, and forestry jobs?

About 429,000 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
