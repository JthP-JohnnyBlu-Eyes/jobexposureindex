Questions about installation, maintenance, and repair jobs and AI

Will AI replace installation, maintenance, and repair jobs?

Not as a whole. The 50 installation, maintenance, and repair jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 14 out of 100 (low), #18 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which installation, maintenance, and repair jobs are most exposed to AI?

First-Line Supervisors of Mechanics, Installers, and Repairers (41/100), Computer, Automated Teller, and Office Machine Repairers (37/100), Electrical and Electronics Repairers, Powerhouse, Substation, and Relay (34/100).

Which installation, maintenance, and repair jobs are safest from AI?

Tire Repairers and Changers (0/100), Riggers (0/100), Refractory Materials Repairers, Except Brickmasons (0/100).

How many people work in installation, maintenance, and repair jobs?

About 6,076,810 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
