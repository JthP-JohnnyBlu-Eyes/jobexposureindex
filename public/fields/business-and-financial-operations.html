Questions about business and financial operations jobs and AI

Will AI replace business and financial operations jobs?

Not as a whole. The 48 business and financial operations jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 53 out of 100 (elevated), #3 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which business and financial operations jobs are most exposed to AI?

Financial Quantitative Analysts (69/100), Compliance Officers (68/100), Human Resources Specialists (64/100).

Which business and financial operations jobs are safest from AI?

Farm Labor Contractors (14/100), Coroners (34/100), Management Analysts (40/100).

How many people work in business and financial operations jobs?

About 18,874,650 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
