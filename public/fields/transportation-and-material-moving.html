Questions about transportation and material moving jobs and AI

Will AI replace transportation and material moving jobs?

Not as a whole. The 52 transportation and material moving jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 22 out of 100 (moderate), #15 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which transportation and material moving jobs are most exposed to AI?

Traffic Technicians (46/100), Transportation Vehicle, Equipment and Systems Inspectors, Except Aviation (46/100), Transportation Inspectors (44/100).

Which transportation and material moving jobs are safest from AI?

Wellhead Pumpers (0/100), Motorboat Operators (0/100), Hoist and Winch Operators (0/100).

How many people work in transportation and material moving jobs?

About 18,501,380 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
