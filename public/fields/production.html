Questions about production jobs and AI

Will AI replace production jobs?

Not as a whole. The 107 production jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 13 out of 100 (low), #21 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which production jobs are most exposed to AI?

Computer Numerically Controlled Tool Programmers (80/100), Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers (47/100), Prepress Technicians and Workers (44/100).

Which production jobs are safest from AI?

Slaughterers and Meat Packers (0/100), Pourers and Casters, Metal (0/100), Paper Goods Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders (0/100).

How many people work in production jobs?

About 8,667,450 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
