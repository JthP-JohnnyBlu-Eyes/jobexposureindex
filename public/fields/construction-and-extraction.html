Questions about construction and extraction jobs and AI

Will AI replace construction and extraction jobs?

Not as a whole. The 61 construction and extraction jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 7 out of 100 (low), #22 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which construction and extraction jobs are most exposed to AI?

Solar Energy Installation Managers (43/100), Energy Auditors (43/100), First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers (40/100).

Which construction and extraction jobs are safest from AI?

Terrazzo Workers and Finishers (0/100), Tapers (0/100), Stonemasons (0/100).

How many people work in construction and extraction jobs?

About 7,844,860 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
