Questions about protective service jobs and AI

Will AI replace protective service jobs?

Not as a whole. The 26 protective service jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 29 out of 100 (moderate), #13 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which protective service jobs are most exposed to AI?

Intelligence Analysts (53/100), Customs and Border Protection Officers (45/100), Gambling Surveillance Officers and Gambling Investigators (44/100).

Which protective service jobs are safest from AI?

Bailiffs (4/100), Lifeguards, Ski Patrol, and Other Recreational Protective Service Workers (8/100), Crossing Guards and Flaggers (11/100).

How many people work in protective service jobs?

About 4,655,290 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
