Questions about management jobs and AI

Will AI replace management jobs?

Not as a whole. The 56 management jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 46 out of 100 (elevated), #7 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which management jobs are most exposed to AI?

Purchasing Managers (59/100), Gambling Managers (57/100), Natural Sciences Managers (57/100).

Which management jobs are safest from AI?

Biofuels Production Managers (29/100), Facilities Managers (32/100), Biomass Power Plant Managers (33/100).

How many people work in management jobs?

About 18,119,840 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
