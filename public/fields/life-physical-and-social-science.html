Questions about life, physical, and social science jobs and AI

Will AI replace life, physical, and social science jobs?

Not as a whole. The 60 life, physical, and social science jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 46 out of 100 (elevated), #8 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which life, physical, and social science jobs are most exposed to AI?

Physicists (75/100), Bioinformatics Scientists (71/100), Social Science Research Assistants (67/100).

Which life, physical, and social science jobs are safest from AI?

Nuclear Technicians (14/100), Nuclear Monitoring Technicians (23/100), Agricultural Technicians (32/100).

How many people work in life, physical, and social science jobs?

About 2,085,140 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
