Questions about computer and mathematical jobs and AI

Will AI replace computer and mathematical jobs?

Not as a whole. The 36 computer and mathematical jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 74 out of 100 (high), #1 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which computer and mathematical jobs are most exposed to AI?

Mathematicians (100/100), Blockchain Engineers (97/100), Computer Programmers (95/100).

Which computer and mathematical jobs are safest from AI?

Health Informatics Specialists (48/100), Actuaries (50/100), Information Technology Project Managers (57/100).

How many people work in computer and mathematical jobs?

About 10,176,730 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
