Questions about healthcare practitioners and technical jobs and AI

Will AI replace healthcare practitioners and technical jobs?

Not as a whole. The 89 healthcare practitioners and technical jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 34 out of 100 (moderate), #11 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which healthcare practitioners and technical jobs are most exposed to AI?

Health Information Technologists and Medical Registrars (66/100), Medical Records Specialists (62/100), Hospitalists (57/100).

Which healthcare practitioners and technical jobs are safest from AI?

Surgical Assistants (4/100), Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (4/100), Prosthodontists (5/100).

How many people work in healthcare practitioners and technical jobs?

About 27,431,810 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
