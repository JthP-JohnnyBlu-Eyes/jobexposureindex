Questions about arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media jobs and AI

Will AI replace arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media jobs?

Not as a whole. The 40 arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 47 out of 100 (elevated), #6 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media jobs are most exposed to AI?

Court Reporters and Simultaneous Captioners (96/100), Poets, Lyricists and Creative Writers (89/100), Interpreters and Translators (88/100).

Which arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media jobs are safest from AI?

Athletes and Sports Competitors (0/100), Dancers (2/100), Lighting Technicians (16/100).

How many people work in arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media jobs?

About 2,458,570 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
