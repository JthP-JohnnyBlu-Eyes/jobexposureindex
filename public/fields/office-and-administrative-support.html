Questions about office and administrative support jobs and AI

Will AI replace office and administrative support jobs?

Not as a whole. The 51 office and administrative support jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 61 out of 100 (high), #2 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which office and administrative support jobs are most exposed to AI?

Proofreaders and Copy Markers (98/100), Correspondence Clerks (96/100), Telephone Operators (89/100).

Which office and administrative support jobs are safest from AI?

Postal Service Mail Sorters, Processors, and Processing Machine Operators (10/100), Mail Clerks and Mail Machine Operators, Except Postal Service (22/100), Postal Service Mail Carriers (23/100).

How many people work in office and administrative support jobs?

About 17,486,450 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
