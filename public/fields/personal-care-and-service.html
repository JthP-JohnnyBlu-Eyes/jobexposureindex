Questions about personal care and service jobs and AI

Will AI replace personal care and service jobs?

Not as a whole. The 31 personal care and service jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 20 out of 100 (moderate), #16 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which personal care and service jobs are most exposed to AI?

Concierges (47/100), First-Line Supervisors of Gambling Services Workers (43/100), First-Line Supervisors of Personal Service Workers (41/100).

Which personal care and service jobs are safest from AI?

Exercise Trainers and Group Fitness Instructors (1/100), Embalmers (4/100), Crematory Operators (4/100).

How many people work in personal care and service jobs?

About 3,746,580 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
