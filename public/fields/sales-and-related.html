Questions about sales and related jobs and AI

Will AI replace sales and related jobs?

Not as a whole. The 22 sales and related jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 46 out of 100 (elevated), #9 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which sales and related jobs are most exposed to AI?

Telemarketers (81/100), Sales Representatives of Services, Except Advertising, Insurance, Financial Services, and Travel (63/100), Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Except Technical and Scientific Products (62/100).

Which sales and related jobs are safest from AI?

Demonstrators and Product Promoters (13/100), Cashiers (21/100), Models (23/100).

How many people work in sales and related jobs?

About 13,615,900 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
