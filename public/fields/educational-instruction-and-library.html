Questions about educational instruction and library jobs and AI

Will AI replace educational instruction and library jobs?

Not as a whole. The 62 educational instruction and library jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 44 out of 100 (elevated), #10 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which educational instruction and library jobs are most exposed to AI?

Library Technicians (57/100), Recreation and Fitness Studies Teachers, Postsecondary (56/100), Tutors (54/100).

Which educational instruction and library jobs are safest from AI?

Special Education Teachers, Preschool (20/100), Preschool Teachers, Except Special Education (23/100), Kindergarten Teachers, Except Special Education (23/100).

How many people work in educational instruction and library jobs?

About 11,356,320 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
