Questions about architecture and engineering jobs and AI

Will AI replace architecture and engineering jobs?

Not as a whole. The 56 architecture and engineering jobs in the index have an average AI exposure score of 49 out of 100 (elevated), #5 of 22 fields. Exposure means parts of the work are likely to change, not that the jobs will disappear.

Which architecture and engineering jobs are most exposed to AI?

Computer Hardware Engineers (73/100), Validation Engineers (70/100), Radio Frequency Identification Device Specialists (67/100).

Which architecture and engineering jobs are safest from AI?

Photonics Technicians (24/100), Automotive Engineering Technicians (28/100), Non-Destructive Testing Specialists (29/100).

How many people work in architecture and engineering jobs?

About 6,464,060 people in the US work in the jobs listed here (BLS, May 2025).
