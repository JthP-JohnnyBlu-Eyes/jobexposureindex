Add an AI exposure checker to your site

Let your readers look up how exposed their job is to AI, right on your page. It's free, needs no account, and updates by itself when the index does. Copy the code, paste it where you want the box, and you're done. It works in WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Ghost, Substack-style HTML blocks and anywhere else that accepts HTML.

Option 1: a lookup box for any job

Readers type their job title and see its score. Good for career blogs, HR and recruiting sites, newsletters and school career pages.

Option 2: the score for one job

Shows the score for a single job, for example in an article about that profession. Every job page has its own code at the bottom; this is the one for accountants and auditors.

The fine print. The widget is free for any site, commercial or not. Please keep the source line under it. It loads only the scores from jobexposureindex.com, sets no cookies and doesn't track your readers. Prefer to use the numbers yourself? Download the whole index.
