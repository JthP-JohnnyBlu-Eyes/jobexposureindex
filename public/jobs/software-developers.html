Questions about software developers and AI

Will AI replace software developers?

Probably not entirely, but the job is likely to change a lot. Software Developers score 87 out of 100 (very high exposure), #19 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 18 of the job's 19 tasks, 15 of them on its own, while 1 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which software developers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Analyze information to determine, recommend, and plan installation of a new system or modification of an existing system"; "Analyze user needs and software requirements to determine feasibility of design within time and cost constraints"; "Confer with systems analysts, engineers, programmers and others to design systems and to obtain information on project limitations and capabilities, performance requirements and interfaces".

Which software developers tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Train users to use new or modified equipment".

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 11 of the 19 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as software developers, and what do they earn?

About 1,687,890 people in the US, with median pay of $135,980 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
