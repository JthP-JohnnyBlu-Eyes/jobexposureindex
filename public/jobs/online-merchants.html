Questions about online merchants and AI

Will AI replace online merchants?

Probably not entirely, but the job is likely to change a lot. Online Merchants score 63 out of 100 (high exposure), #79 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 31 of the job's 34 tasks, 13 of them on its own, while 3 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which online merchants tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Create, manage, or automate orders or invoices, using order management or invoicing software"; "Deliver e-mail confirmation of completed transactions and shipment"; "Calculate purchase subtotals, taxes, and shipping costs for submission to customers".

Which online merchants tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Fill customer orders by packaging sold items and documentation for direct shipping or by transferring orders to manufacturers or third-party distributors"; "Receive and process payments from customers, using electronic transaction services"; "Maintain inventory of shipping supplies, such as boxes, labels, tape, bubble wrap, loose packing materials, or tape guns".

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 18 of the 34 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as online merchants, and what do they earn?

About 1,087,090 people in the US (figure for Business Operations Specialists, All Other), with median pay of $83,050 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
