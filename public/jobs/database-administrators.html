Questions about database administrators and AI

Will AI replace database administrators?

Probably not entirely, but the job is likely to change a lot. Database Administrators score 94 out of 100 (very high exposure), #8 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 18 of the job's 18 tasks, 16 of them on its own. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which database administrators tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Test programs or databases, correct errors, and make necessary modifications"; "Plan, coordinate, and implement security measures to safeguard information in computer files against accidental or unauthorized damage, modification or disclosure"; "Modify existing databases and database management systems or direct programmers and analysts to make changes".

Which database administrators tasks are hardest for AI?

Every task in this job has some AI exposure, though 2 of them need extra software built around the AI.

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 10 of the 18 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as database administrators, and what do they earn?

About 69,990 people in the US, with median pay of $104,620 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
