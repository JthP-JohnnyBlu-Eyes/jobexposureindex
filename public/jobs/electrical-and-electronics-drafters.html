Questions about electrical and electronics drafters and AI

Will AI replace electrical and electronics drafters?

Not entirely. Parts of the job are likely to change as AI takes over routine tasks. Electrical and Electronics Drafters score 57 out of 100 (elevated exposure), #111 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 28 of the job's 30 tasks, 7 of them on its own, while 2 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which electrical and electronics drafters tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Confer with engineering staff and other personnel to resolve problems"; "Study work order requests to determine type of service, such as lighting or power, demanded by installation"; "Determine the order of work and the method of presentation, such as orthographic or isometric drawing".

Which electrical and electronics drafters tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Select drill size to drill test head, according to test design and specifications, and submit guide layout to designated department"; "Supervise or train other technologists, technicians, or drafters".

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 8 of the 30 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as electrical and electronics drafters, and what do they earn?

About 17,920 people in the US, with median pay of $76,870 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023).
