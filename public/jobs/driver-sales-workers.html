Questions about driver/sales workers and AI

Will AI replace driver/sales workers?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Driver/Sales Workers score 33 out of 100 (moderate exposure), #490 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 5 of the job's 12 tasks, 2 of them on its own, while 7 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which driver/sales workers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Record sales or delivery information on daily sales or delivery record"; "Write customer orders and sales contracts according to company guidelines".

Which driver/sales workers tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Drive trucks to deliver such items as food, medical supplies, or newspapers"; "Maintain trucks and food-dispensing equipment and clean inside of machines that dispense food or beverages"; "Arrange merchandise and sales promotion displays or issue sales promotion materials to customers".

Are people already using AI for this work?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
