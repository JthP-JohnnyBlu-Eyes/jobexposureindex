Questions about automotive service technicians and mechanics and AI

Will AI replace automotive service technicians and mechanics?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Automotive Service Technicians and Mechanics score 8 out of 100 (low exposure), #776 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 4 of the job's 30 tasks, 1 of them on its own, while 26 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which automotive service technicians and mechanics tasks can AI do?

Which automotive service technicians and mechanics tasks are hardest for AI?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
