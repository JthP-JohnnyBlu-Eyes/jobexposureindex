Questions about switchboard operators, including answering service and AI

Will AI replace switchboard operators, including answering service?

Probably not entirely, but the job is likely to change a lot. Switchboard Operators, Including Answering Service score 63 out of 100 (high exposure), #80 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 14 of the job's 19 tasks, 10 of them on its own, while 5 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which switchboard operators, including answering service tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Operate communication systems, such as telephone, switchboard, intercom, two-way radio, or public address"; "Record messages, suggesting rewording for clarity or conciseness"; "Page individuals to inform them of telephone calls, using paging or interoffice communication equipment".

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023).
