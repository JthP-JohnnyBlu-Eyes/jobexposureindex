Questions about stockers and order fillers and AI

Will AI replace stockers and order fillers?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Stockers and Order Fillers score 18 out of 100 (low exposure), #655 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 8 of the job's 30 tasks, 3 of them on its own, while 22 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which stockers and order fillers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Complete order receipts"; "Keep records of out-going orders"; "Keep records on the use or damage of stock or stock-handling equipment".

Which stockers and order fillers tasks are hardest for AI?

Are people already using AI for this work?

How many people work as stockers and order fillers, and what do they earn?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
