Questions about morticians, undertakers, and funeral arrangers and AI

Will AI replace morticians, undertakers, and funeral arrangers?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Morticians, Undertakers, and Funeral Arrangers score 16 out of 100 (low exposure), #673 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 5 of the job's 22 tasks, 2 of them on its own, while 17 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which morticians, undertakers, and funeral arrangers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Obtain information needed to complete legal documents, such as death certificates or burial permits"; "Maintain financial records, order merchandise, or prepare accounts".

Which morticians, undertakers, and funeral arrangers tasks are hardest for AI?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
