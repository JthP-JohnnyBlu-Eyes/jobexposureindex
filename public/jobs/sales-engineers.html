Questions about sales engineers and AI

Will AI replace sales engineers?

Not entirely. Parts of the job are likely to change as AI takes over routine tasks. Sales Engineers score 51 out of 100 (elevated exposure), #207 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 22 of the job's 25 tasks, 4 of them on its own, while 3 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which sales engineers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Create sales or service contracts for products or services"; "Document account activities, generate reports, and keep records of business transactions with customers and suppliers"; "Attend company training seminars to become familiar with product lines".

Which sales engineers tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Visit prospective buyers at commercial, industrial, or other establishments to show samples or catalogs, and to inform them about product pricing, availability, and advantages"; "Attend trade shows and seminars to promote products or to learn about industry developments"; "Arrange for demonstrations or trial installations of equipment".

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 13 of the 25 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as sales engineers, and what do they earn?

About 51,790 people in the US, with median pay of $124,900 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
