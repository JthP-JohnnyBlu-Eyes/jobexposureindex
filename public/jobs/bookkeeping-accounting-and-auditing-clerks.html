Questions about bookkeeping, accounting, and auditing clerks and AI

Will AI replace bookkeeping, accounting, and auditing clerks?

Probably not entirely, but the job is likely to change a lot. Bookkeeping, Accounting, and Auditing Clerks score 80 out of 100 (very high exposure), #27 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 30 of the job's 30 tasks, 17 of them on its own. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which bookkeeping, accounting, and auditing clerks tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Operate computers programmed with accounting software to record, store, and analyze information"; "Check figures, postings, and documents for correct entry, mathematical accuracy, and proper codes"; "Operate 10-key calculators, typewriters, and copy machines to perform calculations and produce documents".

Which bookkeeping, accounting, and auditing clerks tasks are hardest for AI?

Every task in this job has some AI exposure, though 13 of them need extra software built around the AI.

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 16 of the 30 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as bookkeeping, accounting, and auditing clerks, and what do they earn?

About 1,373,680 people in the US, with median pay of $50,670 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
