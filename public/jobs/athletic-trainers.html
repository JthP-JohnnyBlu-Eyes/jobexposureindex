Questions about athletic trainers and AI

Will AI replace athletic trainers?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Athletic Trainers score 28 out of 100 (moderate exposure), #550 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 11 of the job's 23 tasks, 3 of them on its own, while 12 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which athletic trainers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Perform general administrative tasks, such as keeping records or writing reports"; "File athlete insurance claims and communicate with insurance providers"; "Teach sports medicine courses to athletic training students".

Which athletic trainers tasks are hardest for AI?

Are people already using AI for this work?
