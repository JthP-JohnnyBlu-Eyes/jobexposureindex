Questions about floral designers and AI

Will AI replace floral designers?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Floral Designers score 20 out of 100 (moderate exposure), #636 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 5 of the job's 16 tasks, 1 of them on its own, while 11 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which floral designers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Inform customers about the care, maintenance, and handling of various flowers and foliage, indoor plants, and other items".

Which floral designers tasks are hardest for AI?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.
