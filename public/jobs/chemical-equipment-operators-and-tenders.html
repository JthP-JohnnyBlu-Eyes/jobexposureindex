Questions about chemical equipment operators and tenders and AI

Will AI replace chemical equipment operators and tenders?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Chemical Equipment Operators and Tenders score 15 out of 100 (low exposure), #686 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 5 of the job's 23 tasks, 2 of them on its own, while 18 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which chemical equipment operators and tenders tasks can AI do?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023).
