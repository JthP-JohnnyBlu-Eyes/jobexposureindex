Questions about technical writers and AI

Will AI replace technical writers?

Probably not entirely, but the job is likely to change a lot. Technical Writers score 70 out of 100 (high exposure), #45 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 14 of the job's 15 tasks, 7 of them on its own, while 1 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which technical writers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Organize material and complete writing assignment according to set standards regarding order, clarity, conciseness, style, and terminology"; "Maintain records and files of work and revisions"; "Edit, standardize, or make changes to material prepared by other writers or establishment personnel".

Which technical writers tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Confer with customer representatives, vendors, plant executives, or publisher to establish technical specifications and to determine subject material to be developed for publication".

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 10 of the 15 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as technical writers, and what do they earn?

About 45,500 people in the US, with median pay of $90,390 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
