Questions about acute care nurses and AI

Will AI replace acute care nurses?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Acute Care Nurses score 31 out of 100 (moderate exposure), #518 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 16 of the job's 27 tasks, 1 of them on its own, while 11 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which acute care nurses tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Document data related to patients' care, including assessment results, interventions, medications, patient responses, or treatment changes".

Which acute care nurses tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Perform emergency medical procedures, such as basic cardiac life support (BLS), advanced cardiac life support (ACLS), and other condition-stabilizing interventions"; "Manage patients' pain relief and sedation by providing pharmacologic and non-pharmacologic interventions, monitoring patients' responses, and changing care plans accordingly"; "Administer blood and blood product transfusions or intravenous infusions, monitoring patients for adverse reactions".

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 6 of the 27 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as acute care nurses, and what do they earn?

About 3,379,720 people in the US (figure for Registered Nurses), with median pay of $97,550 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
