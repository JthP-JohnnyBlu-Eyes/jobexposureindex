Questions about web developers and AI

Will AI replace web developers?

Probably not entirely, but the job is likely to change a lot. Web Developers score 93 out of 100 (very high exposure), #9 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 29 of the job's 29 tasks, 25 of them on its own. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which web developers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Write supporting code for Web applications or Web sites"; "Design, build, or maintain Web sites, using authoring or scripting languages, content creation tools, management tools, and digital media"; "Back up files from Web sites to local directories for instant recovery in case of problems".

Which web developers tasks are hardest for AI?

Every task in this job has some AI exposure, though 4 of them need extra software built around the AI.

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 20 of the 29 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as web developers, and what do they earn?

About 70,190 people in the US, with median pay of $92,650 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
