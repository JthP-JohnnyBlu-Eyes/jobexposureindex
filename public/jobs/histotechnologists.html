Questions about histotechnologists and AI

Will AI replace histotechnologists?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Histotechnologists score 28 out of 100 (moderate exposure), #545 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 7 of the job's 16 tasks, 2 of them on its own, while 9 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which histotechnologists tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Compile and maintain records of preventive maintenance and instrument performance checks according to schedule and regulations"; "Perform tests by following physician instructions".

Which histotechnologists tasks are hardest for AI?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
