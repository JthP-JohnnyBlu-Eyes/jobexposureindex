Questions about pharmacy technicians and AI

Will AI replace pharmacy technicians?

Not entirely. Parts of the job are likely to change as AI takes over routine tasks. Pharmacy Technicians score 43 out of 100 (elevated exposure), #379 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 11 of the job's 21 tasks, 6 of them on its own, while 10 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which pharmacy technicians tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Receive written prescription or refill requests and verify that information is complete and accurate"; "Enter prescription information into computer databases"; "Answer telephones, responding to questions or requests".

Which pharmacy technicians tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Maintain proper storage and security conditions for drugs"; "Operate cash registers to accept payment from customers"; "Mix pharmaceutical preparations, according to written prescriptions".

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 2 of the 21 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as pharmacy technicians, and what do they earn?

About 471,680 people in the US, with median pay of $45,750 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear.
