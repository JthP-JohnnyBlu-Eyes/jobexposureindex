Questions about retail salespersons and AI

Will AI replace retail salespersons?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Retail Salespersons score 34 out of 100 (moderate exposure), #483 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 15 of the job's 24 tasks, 3 of them on its own, while 9 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which retail salespersons tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Maintain records related to sales"; "Prepare sales slips or sales contracts"; "Estimate cost of repair or alteration of merchandise".

Which retail salespersons tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Greet customers and ascertain what each customer wants or needs"; "Prepare merchandise for purchase or rental"; "Demonstrate use or operation of merchandise".

Are people already using AI for this work?

How many people work as retail salespersons, and what do they earn?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
