Questions about customer service representatives and AI

Will AI replace customer service representatives?

Not entirely. Parts of the job are likely to change as AI takes over routine tasks. Customer Service Representatives score 57 out of 100 (elevated exposure), #121 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 15 of the job's 15 tasks, 2 of them on its own. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which customer service representatives tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Complete contract forms, prepare change of address records, or issue service discontinuance orders, using computers"; "Compare disputed merchandise with original requisitions and information from invoices and prepare invoices for returned goods".

Which customer service representatives tasks are hardest for AI?

Every task in this job has some AI exposure, though 13 of them need extra software built around the AI.

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 7 of the 15 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as customer service representatives, and what do they earn?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
