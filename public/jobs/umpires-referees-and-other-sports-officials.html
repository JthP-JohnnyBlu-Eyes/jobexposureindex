Questions about umpires, referees, and other sports officials and AI

Will AI replace umpires, referees, and other sports officials?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Umpires, Referees, and Other Sports Officials score 31 out of 100 (moderate exposure), #514 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 7 of the job's 16 tasks, 4 of them on its own, while 9 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.
