Questions about animal caretakers and AI

Will AI replace animal caretakers?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Animal Caretakers score 27 out of 100 (moderate exposure), #557 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 7 of the job's 22 tasks, 3 of them on its own, while 15 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which animal caretakers tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Collect and record animal information, such as weight, size, physical condition, treatments received, medications given, and food intake"; "Respond to questions from patrons, and provide information about animals, such as behavior, habitat, breeding habits, or facility activities"; "Answer telephones and schedule appointments".

Which animal caretakers tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Feed and water animals according to schedules and feeding instructions"; "Provide treatment to sick or injured animals, or contact veterinarians to secure treatment"; "Mix food, liquid formulas, medications, or food supplements according to instructions, prescriptions, and knowledge of animal species".

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 4 of the 22 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as animal caretakers, and what do they earn?

About 266,910 people in the US, with median pay of $35,360 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
