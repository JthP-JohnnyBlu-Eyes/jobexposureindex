Questions about computer network support specialists and AI

Will AI replace computer network support specialists?

Probably not entirely, but the job is likely to change a lot. Computer Network Support Specialists score 63 out of 100 (high exposure), #76 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 21 of the job's 26 tasks, 12 of them on its own, while 5 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which computer network support specialists tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Configure security settings or access permissions for groups or individuals"; "Document network support activities"; "Configure wide area network (WAN) or local area network (LAN) routers or related equipment".

Which computer network support specialists tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Back up network data"; "Perform routine maintenance or standard repairs to networking components or equipment"; "Install or repair network cables, including fiber optic cables".

Are people already using AI for this work?

Yes. In April–May 2026, 13 of the 26 tasks showed up in real conversations with Claude, according to the Anthropic Economic Index.

How many people work as computer network support specialists, and what do they earn?

About 146,190 people in the US, with median pay of $76,220 a year (BLS, May 2025).

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
