Questions about remote sensing technicians and AI

Will AI replace remote sensing technicians?

Not entirely. Parts of the job are likely to change as AI takes over routine tasks. Remote Sensing Technicians score 55 out of 100 (elevated exposure), #143 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 22 of the job's 22 tasks, 3 of them on its own. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which remote sensing technicians tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Maintain records of survey data"; "Document methods used and write technical reports containing information collected"; "Develop specialized computer software routines to customize and integrate image analysis".

Which remote sensing technicians tasks are hardest for AI?

Are people already using AI for this work?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
