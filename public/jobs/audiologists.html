Questions about audiologists and AI

Will AI replace audiologists?

Not entirely. Parts of the job are likely to change as AI takes over routine tasks. Audiologists score 45 out of 100 (elevated exposure), #334 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 17 of the job's 22 tasks, 3 of them on its own, while 5 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which audiologists tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Maintain patient records at all stages, including initial and subsequent evaluation and treatment activities"; "Perform administrative tasks, such as managing office functions and finances"; "Provide information to the public on hearing or balance topics".

Which audiologists tasks are hardest for AI?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
