Questions about demonstrators and product promoters and AI

Will AI replace demonstrators and product promoters?

Unlikely. Most of the work is hard for today's AI to speed up. Demonstrators and Product Promoters score 13 out of 100 (low exposure), #720 of 923 US jobs. AI could speed up 6 of the job's 24 tasks, 2 of them on its own, while 18 see little or no speed-up. Exposure means the work is likely to change, not that the job will disappear.

Which demonstrators and product promoters tasks can AI do?

The most exposed tasks include "Prepare or alter presentation contents to target specific audiences"; "Write articles or pamphlets about products".

Which demonstrators and product promoters tasks are hardest for AI?

Tasks with little or no AI speed-up include "Provide product samples, coupons, informational brochures, or other incentives to persuade people to buy products"; "Sell products being promoted and keep records of sales"; "Keep areas neat while working and return items to correct locations following demonstrations".

Are people already using AI for this work?

How to read this score. Exposure measures how much of the work AI could speed up, not whether the job will disappear. A highly exposed job can change shape, with people spending less time on routine tasks and more on the rest. The score counts tasks AI can speed up on its own in full, and tasks that need extra software at half weight.

The ratings come from published research based on GPT-4 (2023). To show what's happening now, each task is also checked against the latest Anthropic Economic Index, which records which tasks people actually use AI for.
