The full index, all 923 US occupations with their AI exposure scores, task counts, real-world AI use, employment and pay, as one spreadsheet. Free for research, journalism, teaching and commercial use, as long as you credit the Job Exposure Index.

What's in it

ColumnMeaning
rank1 is the most exposed job; ties share a rank
occupation, onet_soc_code, fieldThe O*NET occupation, its code and its job field
exposure_score, exposure_level0 to 100, and its band (Low, Moderate, Elevated, High, Very high)
tasks_total, tasks_ai_alone, tasks_ai_with_software, tasks_not_exposedHow many of the job's tasks AI could speed up on its own, with extra software, or not at all
tasks_seen_in_ai_useTasks seen in real conversations with Claude, April–May 2026 (Anthropic Economic Index)
us_employment, median_annual_pay_usdBLS, May 2025; empty where BLS doesn't publish a figure
urlThe job's page on this site
