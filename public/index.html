    How the index works

    Public data, open method.

    Every score is built from published research and official data, and the full method is public, so you can check it yourself.

    1. Start with the tasks.O*NET lists the tasks that make up each US occupation.
    2. Rate each task.Each task is rated on whether AI like GPT-4 could do it at least twice as fast, using published research from OpenAI and the University of Pennsylvania.
    3. Add the job market.Bureau of Labor Statistics data shows how many people do the job and what it pays.
    4. Update as AI changes.Scores are reviewed as new models and new research come out, and the weekly report covers what moved.
