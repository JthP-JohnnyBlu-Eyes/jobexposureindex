Five steps to stay ahead, whatever your score

  1. Look up your own job's tasks. Your job page on jobexposureindex.com lists every task and whether AI can speed it up. Mark the ones you spend most time on.
  2. Use AI on your most exposed tasks first. People who use AI for the routine parts of their job become more productive, and harder to replace.
  3. Grow the tasks AI finds hard. Judgement, working with people, hands-on work and responsibility for outcomes hold their value longest.
  4. Know your nearby options. Each job page lists similar jobs with lower exposure that use many of the same skills.
  5. Check in regularly. AI changes fast. The weekly report tells you when something moves for your field.
